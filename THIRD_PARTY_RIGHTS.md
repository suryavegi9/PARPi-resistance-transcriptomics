# Third-party rights and attribution
Review date: 26 November 2026. Scope: proposed release tree, not a blanket license for everything accessible through linked services.

| Material | Evidence and treatment |
|---|---|
| GEO source matrices, D1-D8 | Official [GEO disclaimer](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/info/disclaimer.html) permits access/distribution by NCBI but reserves submitter IP claims. Exact acquisition links replace redistribution. None of the eleven raw matrices or full SOFT contact records is bundled. Cite each accession and the source publications listed in the dataset manifest. No source-specific blanket relicensing is asserted. |
| Derived Hallmark statistics and core_enrichment fields | Original analysis results retain Hallmark attribution. [MSigDB terms](https://www.gsea-msigdb.org/gsea/msigdb_license_terms.jsp) cover v6-v7.5.1 and v2022.1 onward under CC BY 4.0; copyright Broad Institute, MIT and Regents of the University of California. Hallmark H collections are used; restricted KEGG/BioCarta/ST C2 resources are not bundled. No complete GMT/library is redistributed. Core-enrichment identifiers are derived overlap lists, not relicensed third-party resources. Historical database release was not fully recorded; this is a provenance limitation. |
| Gene identifiers/annotation | Derived identifiers are retained as research facts; no org.Hs.eg.db database or package is bundled. The [official annotation package](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/data/annotation/html/org.Hs.eg.db.html) specifies Artistic-2.0 for the package. This does not grant ownership of underlying source databases. Historical mapping versions remain incompletely recorded. |
| R/Python packages | No package binaries/source distributions are bundled. Install from their official repositories under their own licenses; package versions are session-specific. MIT for repository code does not relicense dependencies. |
| Publication plots/graphical abstract | Author-created research plots and R grid artwork; no publisher page, logo, stock illustration or third-party graphic was identified. Nine PNGs extracted from the author's manuscript preserve artwork bytes; the manuscript itself is excluded. |

MSigDB attribution: Broad Institute, Massachusetts Institute of Technology, and Regents of the University of California; Hallmark collection. Cite Liberzon et al., *The Molecular Signatures Database hallmark gene set collection*, Cell Systems (2015), DOI [10.1016/j.cels.2015.12.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2015.12.004), alongside the accession sources and repository version. For modified derived tables identify the changes and preserve CC BY attribution.

The release does not redistribute the raw matrices or external annotation libraries whose source-specific terms are not established here. No outstanding right to redistribute such excluded material is claimed. The author remains responsible for confirming ownership of original research outputs at final release approval.
