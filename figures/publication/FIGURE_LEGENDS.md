# Publication figure legends

Extracted from the supplied author-review manuscript; wording preserved.

Figure 1. Discovery in GSE235980. (A) PCA of variance-stabilized counts across the two BRCA1 backgrounds. (B) Resistant-versus-sensitive log2 fold-change concordance (ρ = 0.271). (C) Among 915 shared large-effect DEGs, 412 were concordantly upregulated, 280 concordantly downregulated and 223 discordant. (D) NES for the ten shared Hallmark hypotheses frozen for external validation.

Figure 2. External validation of the ten frozen hypotheses: (A) D2, five directional matches and no significant match; (B) D3, one directional match and no significant match; (C) D4, seven directional and five significant matches. (D) D4 robustness to region 440-R3 exclusion and tumor-level pseudobulk analysis (NES ρ = 0.996 and 0.983, respectively). Twelve regions represent four tumors.

Figure 3. Treatment-state analysis in GSE202434. (A) Separate HCC1806 and PSN1 PCAs. (B) H3/P3 NES concordance (ρ = 0.663; 39/50 matching directions). (C) Eighteen robust exposure-associated Hallmarks, requiring concordant significant H3/P3 and preserved P3 direction and significance after replicate exclusion. (D) Selected Hallmarks across H1–H3 and P1–P3. Positive H3/P3 NES favors maintained treatment; P3 also includes phenotypic re-sensitization. Asterisks indicate adjusted P < 0.05.

Figure 4. Robustness, heterogeneity and serial selection. (A) D6 primary versus sensitivity Hallmark NES (ρ = 0.879); (B) D6 frozen-hypothesis validation. (C) D7 MDA-MB-468 versus SUM1315 NES (ρ = 0.377); (D) six concordantly significant Hallmarks. (E) Fourteen robust D8 final-state Hallmarks. (F) Exploratory D8 stage-versus-S2 NES for the 15 primary significant final-state Hallmarks. Asterisks mark adjusted P < 0.05; serial stages contribute no additional accession votes.

Figure 5. Final cross-dataset synthesis. (A) Historical six-accession and final eight-accession classifications of 50 Hallmarks. (B) Ten frozen D1 hypotheses; asterisks indicate significant support under the accession-specific rules, not pooled P values; grey D7 cells are discordant and abstain. (C) Historical nine-unit and final 12-unit sensitivity classifications; units within an accession are not independent studies. (D) Observational ON−OFF mean-NES difference versus mean direct D5 H3/P3 response (ρ = −0.256; 24/50 matching directions). D7 uses maintained-treatment history; D8 is excluded. Tables S1–S3 contain full results.
